1. Product Overview

Build a modern, professional, mobile-first website for a local flower shop:

Business name: Cửa Hàng Hoa Tươi Vũng Tàu
Category: Local Flower Shop / Florist
Google rating: 4.8/5 from 140 reviews
Address: 57 Phạm Hồng Thái, Tam Thắng, Hồ Chí Minh 790000, Việt Nam
Phone: +84 567 804 444
Website: shophoavungtau.com

Primary business goal

The website is NOT primarily an informational website.

Its main purpose is to generate:

Inbound phone calls

Flower-order inquiries

Quote requests

Delivery requests

Event / corporate flower orders

Every major section should guide visitors toward one of these actions.

Primary CTA

Secondary CTA

ĐẶT HOA / NHẬN BÁO GIÁ

2. Target Customers

Design for Vietnamese customers searching for flowers in Vũng Tàu / Hồ Chí Minh, especially people who need flowers quickly.

Main customer groups

People ordering birthday flowers

People sending flowers to a partner/friend/family member

Customers looking for congratulatory flowers

Customers ordering grand-opening flowers

Customers ordering funeral flowers

Customers ordering flowers for conferences and events

Businesses ordering flowers in large quantities

Customers who need same-day/local delivery

Customers who already know what type of flower they want but need fast consultation

The website should communicate:

Beautiful flowers + fresh flowers + reliable delivery + fast consultation.
